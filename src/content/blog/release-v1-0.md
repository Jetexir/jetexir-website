We are thrilled to announce the first stable release of **Jetexir V1.0**, a free, open source enhancement suite for
WooCommerce.

Jetexir V1.0 ships with **17 addons** out of the box. Every addon is designed to work seamlessly with your existing
store, is configurable without a single line of code, and addresses a real day-to-day need: whether that is making
discounts impossible to miss, helping shoppers decide faster, or tidying up how you manage orders behind the scenes.

Scroll through the list below, grouped by product, cart, checkout, order, and customization, and see what each addon
brings to your store.

## Free and open source

Jetexir is completely free and open source. Install it from
the [WordPress plugin repository](https://wordpress.org/plugins/jetexir/) or browse the source and contribute
on [GitHub](https://github.com/Jetexir/jetexir-wp-plugin).

Whether you run a small shop or a growing catalog, Jetexir V1.0 gives you a complete toolkit to elevate both customer
experience and administrative efficiency. Update today, and enjoy a more engaging, professional, and streamlined store.
