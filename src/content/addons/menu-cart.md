Keep the shopping cart always within reach. Menu Cart adds a beautiful cart indicator to your navigation that shows the
live item count at a glance, so customers never have to hunt for their basket.

A dropdown preview gives a quick peek at the current contents, and one click takes shoppers straight to checkout. You
control what the dropdown shows and how it's styled, and it works with any menu position in your theme.
