Word of mouth is one of the most powerful sales tools. Social Sharing adds actionable social icons to your single
product pages so customers can share your products across their networks with a single click.

Choose which platforms appear, control the icon content and styling to fit your design, and even offer a one-click "copy
link" option. Every share brings free, authentic exposure, reinforcing your brand while driving new visitors back to
your store.
