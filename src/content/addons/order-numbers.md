Stop explaining your order numbers to customers. Order Numbers replaces WooCommerce's default random order IDs with
clean, sequential numbers, 1001, 1002, 1003, in exactly the format your business needs.

Add a prefix like "JX-", include the date, or keep it plain: pick a template (number only, prefix & number, date &
number, and more), set your own starting number, and control the increment. Existing orders are reformatted too, so
every order across your store follows the same consistent, professional sequence. With order tracking enabled, you can
pull up any order's details in seconds.
