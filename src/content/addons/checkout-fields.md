Checkout should feel effortless. Checkout Fields gives you complete control over the checkout form. Add the fields your
business needs, edit or rename existing ones, remove the ones you never use, and reorder everything for a natural flow.

Add custom fields conditionally so shoppers only see what's relevant, and validate inputs to keep orders clean and
accurate. A simpler checkout means fewer abandoned carts and better order data from the very start.
