Product labels grab attention, and the right sale badge can be the difference between a browser and a buyer. Jetexir
lets you place striking, customizable sale badges on top of your product images so promotions are impossible to miss.

Show the discount as a bold percentage ("-20%") or as a fixed amount ("Save $12"), choose the badge shape, colors, and
wording, and position it exactly where you want on both shop archives and single product pages. Sale badges apply
automatically to any product on sale, so your storefront always looks polished without manual work.
