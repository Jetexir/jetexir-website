features:
  - Disable WooCommerce's default related products
  - Show related products by category, tag, or attribute
  - Hand-pick specific products to display instead
  - Display related products as a slider
  - Control how many products appear and the layout
---

Displaying the right products at the right moment increases basket size. Related Products gives you full control over
which products appear on your single product pages, by category, tag, attribute, or as a hand-picked selection for each
product.

Replace WooCommerce's default related products with your own choices, present them as a polished slider or a grid, and
guide customers toward complementary items that keep them browsing and buying.
