features:
  - Change the currency symbol for your whole store
  - Set a custom icon for the currency symbol
  - Custom price formatting
  - Localized decimals and thousands separators
---

Every market formats money differently. Currency Symbols lets you replace WooCommerce's default currency symbol with
your own, including a custom icon, and control how prices are formatted across the store.

Set your preferred decimals and thousands separators so every price reads naturally for your local audience, and keep
the entire storefront consistent with the currency presentation your customers expect.
