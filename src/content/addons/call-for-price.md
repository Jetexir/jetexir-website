features:
  - Display a "Call for price" button on the product page
  - Custom text for products with empty, zero, or out-of-stock prices
  - Automatically hide the sale tag on products with empty prices
  - Perfect for quote-based and B2B catalogues
---

Not every store shows prices publicly. Call for Price lets you replace the price and add-to-cart button with a custom
"Call for price" prompt that links to your contact, booking, or quoting channel.

You can also set distinct messaging for products with empty, zero, or out-of-stock prices, and the sale tag is
automatically hidden whenever no price is displayed. Whether you run a B2B catalogue, sell by negotiation, or offer
quote-based services, your store stays professional and your leads come directly to you.
