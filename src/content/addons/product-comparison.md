Help customers make confident buying decisions with a simple, effective product comparison tool. Shoppers add products
to a comparison table with one click and review features, prices, and specifications side by side on a dedicated page.

You decide how it works: which attributes get compared, what the buttons say, and how the buttons and compare page look.
Comparing options side by side reduces hesitation and gets customers to a purchase faster.
