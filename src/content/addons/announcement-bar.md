Turn your store's top into a marketing channel. The Announcement Bar displays fully customizable messages, flash sales,
free shipping thresholds, or brand updates, right where customers can't miss them.

Create as many bars as you need, each with its own content and style, and control exactly where each one appears with
display conditions based on pages, categories, or products. Simple to configure, easy to keep on brand, and always in
front of the right shoppers.

## Developer hooks

### Shortcodes

- **`[jetexir_announcement_bar]`** — Renders a saved announcement by its `code` attribute.
