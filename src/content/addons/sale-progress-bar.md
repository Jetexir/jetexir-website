Turn a sale into a compelling story. The Sale Progress Bar displays a live progress indicator on products that are on
sale, showing exactly how many units have been sold and how many are still available.

Shoppers act when they sense scarcity. Seeing "32 of 50 sold" next to a discounted product creates genuine urgency,
encouraging customers to buy now instead of coming back later and finding it gone. The bar appears automatically on
products on sale, and works perfectly for flash sales, seasonal discounts, and limited-stock launches.
