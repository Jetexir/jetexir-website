---
title: Price Variations
category: Product
icon: price-variation
summary: Show variable product prices the way you want, with ranges, single prices, or clear "From" prefixes.
order: 2
features:
  - Choose from multiple variation price display formats
  - Show only the lowest or only the highest price
  - Show price as a range, minimum to maximum or maximum to minimum
  - Add a "From:" prefix before the minimum price
  - Add an "Up To:" prefix before the maximum price
  - Remove the "clear" link from the price display
---

By default, WooCommerce shows variable products as a confusing range, "$49 – $99", which leaves customers guessing what
they'll actually pay. Price Variations puts you in control of exactly how those prices are presented on both shop and
product pages.

Display the full range in either direction, show just the lowest or just the highest price, or clarify the range with a
"From:" or "Up To:" prefix. Every format keeps your pricing transparent, builds trust, and removes the guesswork from
the buying decision.
